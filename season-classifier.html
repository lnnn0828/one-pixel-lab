How do we measure “close”? · 如何计算“相似”？

Distance = √[(month gap ÷ 3)² + (temperature gap ÷ 15)²]. A 3-month gap counts the same as a 15°C gap. Months wrap around: December and January are only one month apart.距离 = √[(月份差 ÷ 3)² + (温差 ÷ 15)²]。相差3个月与相差15°C的影响相同。月份循环计算：12月和1月只差1个月。

This is a 3-nearest-neighbor classifier. It remembers labeled examples and votes; it does not train weights. If votes tie, the tied label with the nearest example wins. Exact distance ties follow the table order.这是“3近邻”分类器：记住有标签的例子，再投票，不需要训练权重。票数相同时，选择拥有最近例子的标签；距离完全相同时按下表顺序决定。

A guess from examples, not a calendar rule. · 这是根据例子的猜测，不是日历规则。
Real seasons follow calendar or astronomical definitions, and temperatures vary by location. An unusually warm January may confuse this toy model. Vote counts are evidence, not probabilities. Try keeping the month fixed while changing temperature, then keeping temperature fixed while changing month.实际季节按日历或天文定义划分，气温也因地区而异。异常温暖的1月可能让这个小模型困惑。票数是证据，不是概率。试着固定月份改变气温，再固定气温改变月份。
